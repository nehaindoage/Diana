Diana Glow Studio ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes our general practices for information shared through our website and communications. The exact information collected depends on the features and services you use.

1. Information We May Collect

When you contact us or inquire about a service, you may choose to share information such as:

  • Your name and contact details, including your phone number.
  • Booking preferences, requested services, preferred dates, and details included in your messages.
  • Feedback or other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser or device details, and pages visited, if collected through website tools or service providers.

Please avoid sending sensitive or unnecessary personal information through general inquiries.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and booking inquiries.
  • Discuss makeup, hairstyling, bridal, and other requested beauty services.
  • Coordinate appointments and provide relevant updates.
  • Respond to feedback and improve our services and website.
  • Maintain website security and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp, Instagram & Third-Party Services

Our website may link to WhatsApp, Instagram, or other third-party websites to help you contact us. If you use those services, the information you provide is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms. We do not control how third-party platforms collect or process information.

4. Cookies & Website Technologies

The website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, or performance measurement if such tools are enabled. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. Where required by law, consent will be requested for non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

5. How Information May Be Shared

We may share relevant information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to inquiries, or coordinate requested services. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, users, or security. We do not intend to sell your personal information.

6. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including applicable business and legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have shared with us, or ask questions about how it is handled. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and some information may need to be retained where legally required.

8. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites and services. Those services operate independently and have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal obligations. Any updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact Diana Glow Studio.
